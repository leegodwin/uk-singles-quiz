          30th December 2024

          This is such a sad chart.

          Almost a quarter of it is more than 5 years old and either had no massive virality this year or is a Christmas song. Only Murder on the Dancefloor and Unwritten had the kind of year deserving of placing them on a 2024 year end list imo. Everything else is unnecessary and robbing songs that peaked higher and were more defining this year than most of the random older stuff festering in the latter half of this chart.

          It's a shame the ACR system doesn't prevent these old songs from being here like Billboard's recurrency rules do. Because there's no way Mr Brightside is a song people remember for being a big 2024 hit. And every year we get more songs like it until in about a decade there'll be no 2034 songs on the year end list

          Outside of that, really great year for music on and off the charts :)
